### What of Tabler is NOT used

- No file from `@tabler/core/dist` is used or shipped. This includes `dist/libs`, which bundles third-party plugins
  (for example ApexCharts) under their own, different licences. Tabler's prebuilt stylesheets and fonts are not shipped either.
- No Tabler JavaScript is shipped.
- No web font is loaded from Google Fonts or any other external host.

This is enforced, not just intended: the Tabler build in `scripts/build-theme.js` fails when the Sass compile loads any source
outside `@tabler/core/scss` and `src/theme`, and when the compiled stylesheet mentions Google Fonts, ApexCharts or `dist/libs`.
